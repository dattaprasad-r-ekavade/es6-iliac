An endless-mine roguelike. Around twenty minutes a run.

Go down. Kill what rises out of the floor. Every room you clear is worth more than the last, and every shut door asks you the same question: bank what you are holding, or open it.

Die and the stones are gone. But the next one down keeps your rank, half your pack, and knows exactly where your body is.

The loop

  • Buy a mine. The first is free. Harder ones cost the same jiva stones you go down to fetch — 8, 24, 48, 80.
  • Clear rooms. Room one pays. Room two pays more. The door does not open while anything in the room is still standing.
  • Choose, at every door. Camp and the stones are banked and the run ends. Press on and the next room is worth more — but dying loses the lot.
  • Spend what you carried out. On a harder mine, or on gear at the stall. Gear is the only thing a death cannot take from you.

Controls

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · Shift sprint · Space jump
  • Left click attack · Right click guard (one-handed only)
  • Q cast the readied spell · 4 5 6 7 8 choose which one
  • E talk, open, take · I character and pack · B trade · J journal
  • F1 full controls · Esc back · F5 / F9 save and load

What I actually want to know

At the shut door — did you ever actually hesitate?

That decision is the whole game, and it is the one thing I cannot find out by watching. If it never made you stop and think, please say so. That is the single most useful sentence you can send me, and it is worth more than a list of bugs.

Other things worth a line: where you got confused, what you expected a key to do that it did not, and the room number you died in.

A note on recordings

The game asks once, on first launch, whether it may send anonymous recordings of how you played — where you went, what you fought, how long you spent at each door. It is a straight yes or no. Nothing is sent if you decline, and the game plays exactly the same either way.

There is no account, no email, no name, and no IP logging. Each install gets a random identifier and nothing else.

Early alpha

This is a prototype in active development, built solo. Expect rough edges. Windows only, no installer — unzip and run.
